# BirdPlot Trademark Policy

The BirdPlot **source code** is open source under the Apache License 2.0.
The **name "BirdPlot"** and the **BirdPlot logo** are not covered by that
license (Apache 2.0, section 6). They belong to the original project,
created by x0root.

## What you can do

- Fork, modify, and redistribute the code under the Apache 2.0 license.
- Say that your project is **"Based on BirdPlot by x0root"** and link to the
  original repository. This credit is welcome, and we ask that you keep it
  visible in your README and in your store listing.
- Refer to BirdPlot by name when describing compatibility or comparing,
  as long as you don't imply it is your product or endorsed by us.

## What you can't do

- Publish a modified version under the name BirdPlot, or under a name or
  logo confusingly similar (for example "BirdPlot Pro", "Bird-Plot",
  "BirdPIot").
- Use the BirdPlot logo or icon as your own branding.
- Present your fork as the official BirdPlot extension, or imply that
  x0root or the BirdPlot project made or endorses it.
- Use the BirdPlot name in a store listing, domain, or social account in a
  way that could mislead people about who publishes it.

## Official releases

Only builds published by the original project count as BirdPlot:

- The official GitHub repository
- The official listings on the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons (AMO)

If you distribute a modified version, rename it and keep the attribution
line above.
